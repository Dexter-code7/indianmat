Sree Narayana Guru's Darshanam and Ezhava Marriage FAQ

What is Sree Narayana Guru's main teaching about marriage and society?

Guru taught "One Caste, One Religion, One God for Mankind," meaning marriages and social life should be based on equality, not caste discrimination.

Is dowry required in Ezhava marriages?

No. Sree Narayana Guru and SNDP have always discouraged dowry, promoting marriages based on mutual respect instead.
