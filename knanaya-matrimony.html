Knanaya Matrimony FAQ

What is Knanaya Matrimony?

Knanaya Matrimony refers to matchmaking within the Knanaya community — a historically endogamous Kerala Christian community descended from Jewish-Christian migrants who arrived in 345 AD under the merchant Thomas of Cana (Knai Thoma). India Weddings Matrimony offers a dedicated Knanaya Matrimony service for Knanaya Catholic, Jacobite and Orthodox families across Kerala and the diaspora.

Who are the Knanaya people and what is their history?

The Knanaya trace their origin to about 345 AD, when Jewish-Christian families led by Thomas of Cana migrated to Cranganore (Kodungallur), Kerala, to reinforce the Christian community first founded by St. Thomas the Apostle around 52 AD. To preserve their distinct lineage, the community adopted endogamy, a tradition that continues today.
